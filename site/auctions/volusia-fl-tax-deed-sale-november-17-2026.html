Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Volusia County, Florida
  • Sale date and time: Tuesday, November 17, 2026 at 9:00 a.m.1 (the Clerk runs sales at 9:00 AM ET2)
  • Where: Online at www.volusia.realtaxdeed.com12
  • Clerk Tax Deed Department: Volusia County Courthouse, 101 N. Alabama Avenue, Room C119, DeLand, Florida; (386) 736-59192
  • Sale order: Each item is auctioned in order of certificate number and sold to the highest bidder2
  • Notice: Each sale is advertised once a week for four consecutive weeks in The West Volusia Beacon2
  • Opening bid: The sum of outstanding tax certificates, delinquent taxes paid, fees, costs and interest; subject to change without notice2
  • Statute: Chapter 197, Florida Statutes, and Department of Revenue rule 12D-13.060 onward (per the Clerk)2

Certificates drop off the sale when owners redeem, which they can do until the high bidder’s full payment is received. Re-check the Clerk Tax Deed Inquiry listing before you bid.2
