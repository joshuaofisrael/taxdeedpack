Bidder rules from the Clerk

  • Auctions are in person in the Courthouse front lobby in Port St. Joe.2
  • The Clerk will not maintain escrow accounts; bring cash or certified funds for a five (5) percent winning bid deposit. You will not be allowed to leave the courthouse to obtain these funds.2
  • Tax deed sales and issuance of tax deeds are governed by Chapter 197, Florida Statutes.2
  • All properties sold at a tax deed sale qualify under “buyer beware.” Purchase of a Tax Deed does not warrant or guarantee clear and marketable title.2
  • The Property Information Report the Clerk receives is only a brief record search for general information purposes.2
