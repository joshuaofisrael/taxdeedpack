Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Seminole County, Florida (county seat Sanford)
  • Sale date and time: Thursday, November 12, 2026 at 9:00 a.m., “or any subsequently scheduled sale date”123
  • Who runs it: The Seminole County Clerk of the Circuit Court and Comptroller, which is statutorily responsible for tax deed sales under Chapter 197, Florida Statutes4
  • Where: Online at seminole.realtaxdeed.com; tax deed case files are on the Clerk’s RealTDM site14
  • How sales start: A tax certificate holder applies for a tax deed through the Tax Collector; no court case is filed4
  • Clerk mailing address: Post Office Box 8099, Sanford, FL 32772-8099; tax deed questions by phone at 407-665-4300 for older files4

Every certificate is sold only if it is not redeemed first, and the notices allow the sale to move to a later scheduled date, so parcels can drop off or shift. Re-check the auction calendar before you bid.1
