Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Marion County, Florida (Ocala)
  • Sale date and time: Wednesday, December 9, 2026, scheduled to begin at 9:00 a.m. E.S.T.123
  • Where: Online at https://marion.realtaxdeed.com (RealAuction)15
  • Clerk Tax Deeds office: (AB) 1st Floor, Room 125; (352) 671-56485
  • Notice: Published in the Voice of South Marion once a week for four consecutive weeks, with the sale at least 30 days after first publication (F.S. 197.512)5
  • Opening bid: All delinquent taxes, penalties, advertising charges, tax search costs and fees paid by the applicant, plus certificates sold after the application, omitted taxes and current taxes4
  • Address: Not provided by the Clerk; the legal description on the certificate is the basis for the application4
  • Statute: Chapter 197, Florida Statutes, and Florida Administrative Code Chapter 12D (per the Clerk)4

Items with status REDEEM, REMOVE or RESCHED in the Clerk’s tax deed search by sale date will not be sold on the scheduled date. Re-check before you bid.4

Deposit and payment rules from the Clerk

Step Rule Deadline for the Dec 9 sale (our reading)

Deposit methodWire transfer (include a $6.00 wire fee), cashier’s check, certified check or money order; no personal checks4Same Monday cutoff4
Winning bid deposit$200 or 5% of the bid, whichever is greater, locked in your account and non refundable4Immediately after each property sells4
Balance, recording fees, documentary stamp feesCertified check, wire transfer (plus $6) or cashier’s check only; no personal, business or trustee account checks or credit cards4Within 24 hours after the auction (by about 9:00 a.m. Thursday, Dec 10 at the earliest item)4
  • Foreclosure deposits are kept separately from tax deed accounts, but an existing RealAuction foreclosure registration does not need to re-register.4
  • Under F.S. 197.542(2) the Clerk may refuse the bid of anyone who previously bid and did not honor it, and Marion enforces this.4
  • All properties are sold “AS IS”, buyer beware, with no warranty as to title, liens, access, zoning, improvements or utilities.4
  • Governmental liens not satisfied in full from surplus survive the tax deed, and the land is sold subject to current taxes.4

The weekday dates in the third column are our calculation from the Clerk’s rules for a Wednesday sale. Confirm exact cutoffs with the Clerk.
