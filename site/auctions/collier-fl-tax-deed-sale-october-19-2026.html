Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Collier County, Florida
  • Sale date and time: Monday, October 19, 2026 at 1:00 p.m.1
  • Location: Collier County Courthouse, 1st Floor, Multi-Purpose Room1, at the Collier County Government complex2
  • Clerk office address: 3315 Tamiami Trail East, Ste. 102, Naples, FL 341122
  • Usual schedule: Auctions are held on Mondays as needed at 1:00 p.m. (subject to change)2
  • Notice period: The Clerk publishes each Tax Deed legal notice on its website for four consecutive weeks before the auction2
  • Statute: Chapter 197, Florida Statutes (per the Clerk)2

Certificates drop off the sale when owners redeem. Re-check the Clerk upcoming sales list before you travel or bid.3

Bidder rules from the Clerk

  • You must be present at the auction or send a representative. No phone calls or electronic bids are allowed.2
  • Arrive in person fifteen (15) minutes before the sale. You cannot register or bid by mail or internet.2
  • The successful bidder must provide a non refundable deposit of 5% of the bid or $200, whichever is greater, at the time of the sale.2
  • The balance is due in guaranteed funds (cash or cashier’s check) within 24 hours, excluding weekends and legal holidays, along with recording fees including documentary stamps. If full payment is not made, the Clerk cancels all bids and re-advertises the sale.2
  • IRS Form 8300 is required for cash payments over $10,000, as defined on the Clerk page.2
  • “Buyer Beware”: the tax deed does not guarantee clear and marketable title, parcels are sold as is, and the Property Information Report is not to be relied upon as a title search.2
