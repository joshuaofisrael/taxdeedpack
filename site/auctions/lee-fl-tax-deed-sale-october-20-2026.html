Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Lee County, Florida
  • Sale date on published notices: October 20, 2026 (10/20/2026) at 10:00 a.m.1
  • Usual sale schedule (Clerk): Tuesdays, starting at 10:00 a.m. online2
  • Auction site: lee.realtaxdeed.com12
  • Conducted by: Kevin C. Karnes, Lee County Clerk of the Circuit Court and Comptroller (named on the published notices)1
  • Statute: Florida Statute Chapter 197 (sales per F.S. 197.542 on the Clerk FAQ)3

Parcel lists change when owners redeem. Always re-check the Clerk list and RealAuction calendar on sale day.

Bidder rules from the Clerk

  • Register on the RealAuction site and place a valid deposit before bids are considered.3
  • Deposit: $200 or 5 percent of the maximum bid, whichever is greater, for each item you anticipate winning.3
  • Full payment for a winning bid is due by 1:00 p.m. the business day following the sale (wire, cash, cashier's check, or money order), including recording fees and documentary stamps of $0.70 per $100 of the bid.3
  • If full payment is not made by that deadline, the deposit is forfeited and you may be barred from future tax deed sales.3
  • A tax deed does not guarantee clear title; other liens and encumbrances may survive. Research before bidding.3
