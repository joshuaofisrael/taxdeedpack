What to check before you bid

  • Confirm the case is still active on the auction calendar the morning of the sale; redeemed and cancelled files drop off.56
  • Open the Notice of Application for Tax Deed and the full Tax Deed Record linked from each case on the auction site.5
  • Look up the parcel with the Sarasota County Property Appraiser and pull the Official Records for liens, mortgages and code enforcement that may survive.
  • Set your deeding name and complete mailing address before the sale; the Clerk warns that wrong deeding details can delay recording and add fees.5
  • Line up wire funds in advance so you can meet the balance deadline in your confirmation email.5
