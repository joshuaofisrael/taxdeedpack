Sale facts (sourced)

  • County: Hillsborough County, Florida
  • Sale date on published notices: October 15, 2026 (10/15/2026)1
  • Usual sale time (Clerk FAQ): Thursdays at 10:00 a.m. online2
  • Auction site: hillsborough.realtaxdeed.com12
  • Conducted by: Hillsborough County Clerk of the Circuit Court (Victor D. Crist named on the published notices)12
  • Statute: Florida Statute Chapter 1972

Parcel lists change when owners redeem. Always re-check the Clerk list and RealAuction calendar on sale day.

Bidder rules from the Clerk

  • Research properties before bidding. The Clerk does not advise on tax deed sales and lists folio numbers, not structure condition.2
  • Deposit before bidding: 5% of the bid or $200, whichever is greater (nonrefundable).2
  • Full payment, including documentary stamps and recording fees, is due by 10:00 a.m. the next business day (online, or cash/cashier’s check to the Clerk).2
  • If full payment is not made, the Clerk cancels bids, re-advertises, and applies the deposit to costs; remaining funds may apply to the opening bid.2
