Privacy Policy

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC operates taxdeedpack.com and the related order API. This policy describes information collected to take an order, take payment, deliver a PDF, and track optional referrals.

Information we collect

  • Buyer name and email
  • Property street address, city, county, state, and/or APN
  • Requested research sections and the disclaimer acknowledgment
  • Optional referral code
  • Order status, timestamps, and Stripe Checkout identifiers

Payment cards are processed by Stripe. We receive payment status and identifiers, not full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To create a Stripe Checkout Session for $149 USD
  • To generate and email the research pack PDF
  • To record referrals (code, order id, amount, timestamp)
  • To operate the authenticated operations view and resend email

Processors

Depending on deployment: GitHub Pages (marketing site), Cloudflare Workers and D1 (API and order records), Stripe (payments), and Resend or a comparable email provider (PDF delivery). Preferred partner sites are independent and have their own policies.

Retention

Order records are kept as needed for fulfillment, bookkeeping, referral tracking, and dispute response. You may email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com to request a copy or correction of your order contact details.

Security

The operations view is password protected. Stripe webhook signatures are verified in production. Do not send card numbers to the operations email.
