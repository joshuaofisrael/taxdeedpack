How we gather research

We use software and AI to gather, extract, and organize information from public records and other identified sources into the Research Pack PDF. Automation can miss or misread a source. Every material fact in the final PDF must still be traceable to an actual source, with numbered footnotes at the bottom. If a source does not establish the fact, the pack shows NOT VERIFIED.

This product is informational research only. It is not an appraisal, title search, legal opinion, or recommendation to bid. Customers keep full responsibility for their own due diligence and transaction decisions.
